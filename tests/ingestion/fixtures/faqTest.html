The following are frequently asked questions for new undergraduate students.

Start off by checking out our Registration Steps page for useful information and videos.

Our returning student page should help.

The breadth requirement is part of most Bachelor of Arts degree programs. Its purpose is to allow you to engage in a variety of topics so you receive a well-rounded arts education. We have prepared a quick video to help explain what this is.

Students whose native language is not English will be required to meet the University English as a Second Language Requirements (ESLR). The English as a Second Language requirement is managed through the School of Linguistics and Language Studies (SLaLS). Visit the SLaLS website for more information about studying with an ESLR.


Information about how to view your grades or exam schedule can be found at the Registrar’s Office website on the Exams and Grades page.
